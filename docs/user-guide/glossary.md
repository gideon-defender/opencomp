# Glossary

- **Access request** — an outsider's request for gated Trust Center materials.
- **Approval** — a second person's confirmation before a policy, task, or
  request counts as done.
- **Auditor view** — read-only OpenComp area built for external auditors.
- **Automation** — a scheduled evidence-collection run attached to a task.
- **Background check** — a Checkr screening of a candidate or employee.
- **Candidate** — a person being screened who is not an employee; screening
  creates no account and grants no access.
- **Check** — one cloud-security rule evaluated against an account.
- **Connection** — a linked third-party account (cloud, tool) that syncs data.
- **Control** — a specific safeguard required by a framework.
- **Custom role** — a role with a chosen permission set, assigned in Gideon.
- **Evidence** — proof attached to a task (file, link, synced data).
- **Finding** — a pentest result, a cloud-check failure, or an auditor-raised
  issue, with a severity.
- **Framework** — a compliance standard activated for the organization
  (SOC 2, ISO 27001, HIPAA, …).
- **Gideon** — the identity service: SSO login and the sole place where
  access and roles are granted.
- **ISMS** — Information Security Management System; the ISO 27001 document
  set.
- **Knowledge base** — curated policies, answers, and documents that power AI
  features.
- **Member** — a person in an organization, synced from Gideon with roles.
- **Offboarding** — the revocation and collection workflow triggered by an
  offboard date.
- **Organization** — the tenant everything belongs to.
- **Policy** — a written company rule with a draft → review → published
  lifecycle.
- **Portal** — the employee-facing app (policies, training, tasks, devices).
- **Remediation** — fixing a cloud finding, singly or in batch.
- **Risk register** — the list of organizational risks with scores and owners.
- **Scan profile** — pentest depth preset (Quick, Standard, Deep, Custom).
- **Secrets manager** — encrypted credential storage in organization settings.
- **SOA** — Statement of Applicability; which controls apply and why.
- **Task** — a unit of compliance work, optionally with evidence.
- **Trust Center** — the public page sharing posture, certifications, and
  documents with customers.
- **Vendor** — a third party in the vendor register with assessment state.
