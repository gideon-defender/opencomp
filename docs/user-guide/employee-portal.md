# Employee portal

A separate, simpler app for employees and contractors (portal-only roles).
Sign in the same way — Gideon SSO — at the portal URL.

## Dashboard

The dashboard shows your compliance standing at a glance: assigned policies
awaiting signature, training to complete, tasks, and device health. Work
top to bottom until it is clear.

## Policies

Open **Policies**, read each assigned policy, and accept it. Acceptance is
timestamped and counts toward the organization's audit evidence — actually
read them.

## Training

Open training to watch assigned videos (including HIPAA training where
applicable). Completion is recorded automatically; download your certificate
afterward if you need it for records.

## Tasks and documents

Complete assigned tasks and fill in company forms. Submissions are tracked;
outstanding items follow you until done.

## Devices

Check device health and Fleet policy status. A healthy
device keeps you compliant without further action.

## Access requests and whistleblower reports

Your administrator can enable an access-request form and a whistleblower
report form in **Settings → Portal**. Use the appropriate form, complete the
required fields, and confirm submission. An access request is a request for
review; it does not immediately grant access to a system.

## If completion does not appear

Reload the portal after saving or finishing training. Check that you used
the correct organization and that the action completed successfully. Give
your administrator the step name and error message if it remains outstanding.
Avoid resubmitting confidential reports just to test whether the page works.

## Practical guidance

- Complete assigned policies and training by their deadlines. Contact your
  manager if a requirement does not apply or cannot be completed.
- Lost your laptop? Tell your manager immediately and follow the device
  steps in [Devices](devices.md).
- You cannot see admin areas from the portal. If you need access to
  something, ask — access is granted in Gideon, never by emailing IT a
  password.
